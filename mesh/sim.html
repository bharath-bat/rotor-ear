RotorEar Sim

Spawns virtual listening phones in this page. Each hears a synthetic drone through a real sound delay (distance ÷ 343 m/s, so arrival times and Doppler are physical), at loudness ∝ 1/distance², plus its own noise. Each runs the real detector and reports to the room's map over the relay, exactly like a phone.
