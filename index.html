    Coverage

    Street map

    Ears can share their GPS too (an opt-in on each phone). Only latitude and longitude numbers go to this screen. GPS only anchors and turns the plan: the pins keep their metre positions.

    Linked watch posts

    Link this screen with other posts' screens by one code. Posts share only track numbers: drone id, position, class, pitch, time. Never audio, never phone data.

    Where this post sits, when there's no GPS · metres and degrees in a plan all posts agree on

    Incident log

    Saved on this phone only. Tap an incident to replay its track on the map.

    Tripwire

    Tap two points on the map. "In" is the side the phones were on when you drew it (Flip changes it). Saved on this phone, per room; the demo has its own lines. A crossing logs where, which way and when. Only positions the phones already worked out are used.

    Drone tags

    Each drone gets a voiceprint from numbers the phones already measure: its rotor pitch and the six fingerprint values. A new drone is matched against these. 80%+ = seen before, 60–79% = possible match (not sure), below = a new tag. Drones of one model can sound alike; a throttle change shifts the pitch. Saved on this phone only; never audio.
