Sound fingerprint

How it works: drone propellers make a steady hum at a "blade-pass" pitch (roughly 100–600 Hz) plus evenly spaced overtones. Rotor Ear scores every candidate pitch by how many of its overtones stand out above the local noise floor, then checks that the same pitch holds for about a second. When it fires, a fingerprint (pitch, rotor peak splitting, beating between rotors, pitch steadiness, sub-harmonic hum, 50/60 Hz lock) is matched against hand-set profiles: quadcopter, fixed-wing, micro drone, or not a drone. Fans, AC units, engines and held musical notes can still look similar. Passive only: it just listens.
